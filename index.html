About Us

About the company

MAR RESOURCE FUL TECNICAL SERVICES L.L.C is a Dubai based HVAC and mechanical contractor. We work with interior design firms and fit-out contractors, and we take a job from the first duct drawing to the final handover.

Our crews supply and install galvanized iron and pre-insulated ducts, chilled water pipes, valves, diffusers, grilles and thermostat connections. We keep every quotation itemised and every payment stage clear, so clients always know what they are paying for.
